FAQ

Common questions

Is it legal to fly a drone over my house?

Yes, when it is done properly. We are registered with the UK Civil Aviation Authority, insured for every flight, and follow the drone rules on distances from people and airspace. If a job needs extra permission, we will tell you before quoting.

Do I need to be at home?

Not always. For most roof jobs we only need your permission and safe access to a take-off spot nearby. We will agree the details when we quote.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Drones can't fly safely in strong wind or rain. If the forecast is poor, we move the flight to the next suitable day at no extra cost.

Are you a chartered surveyor?

No. We provide drone inspections and aerial mapping: clear, detailed visual evidence. If you need a structural survey or professional valuation, we recommend a chartered surveyor, and our photos can help them.

What about my neighbours' privacy?

We only capture what the job needs, avoid filming neighbouring gardens and windows, and delete any incidental footage. Your images are only shared with you.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer after you receive your results. The price is fixed when you book.
